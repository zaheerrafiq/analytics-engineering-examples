\## Supplier Quality Analytics Model



This data model supports supplier quality and risk analysis by consolidating

supplier master data with nonconformance and defect metrics.



\### Modeling Approach

\- Staging models align closely to source systems and apply basic filtering

\- Intermediate models apply business logic and aggregations

\- Fact models expose analytics-ready metrics for BI consumption



\### Key Metrics

\- Defect count

\- Average severity

\- Supplier risk tier



\### Intended Use

These models are designed to support dashboards, scorecards, and

trend analysis for quality, procurement, and operational stakeholders.
